Children's and Educational Apps Privacy Policy

21 children's and educational apps by Dijital Pilot · Last updated: October 3, 2026

In short

  • You do not need to create an account or give a name, email address, age or phone number. We do not ask for them.
  • A child's progress (stars, levels, scores, settings) is kept only on that device. It is not sent to us or to the school.
  • No ads. No tracking. No analytics and no third-party software development kits (SDKs).
  • We do not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos permissions. Only in the Bayraklar (Flags) app, if you turn on the optional daily reminder yourself, a notification permission is requested.
  • Subscription payment and management are handled entirely by Apple. Card details never reach us.
  • In five apps, the Settings screen contacts Apple's public app search service to show an "Our other apps" list. See "Internet connection" below for details.

Data we collect

None of these apps connects to a server, account system or database that we operate, and none sends us personal data. Our App Store "App Privacy" declaration for all 21 apps is Data Not Collected.

Internet connection

The apps can be played without internet. The internet is used for:

  • Showing subscription options, completing a purchase and restoring purchases through Apple's App Store service.
  • Links you tap yourself (this privacy page, Apple's terms of use, the open-source licenses page).
  • The "Our other apps" list (only in İngilizce Alfabe, İlk Okuma, Genel Kültür Kids, Çarpım Tablosu and Bayraklar): When the Settings screen of these five apps opens, and the list stored on the device is older than 24 hours (or does not exist), the app sends one request to Apple's public iTunes search service (itunes.apple.com). The request contains only our developer number, the country code taken from the device's region setting, and the language (Turkish or English). Nothing about the child, the device or how the app is used is added. The reply contains the names, icons and App Store links of our apps; the list is stored on the device and the icons are loaded from Apple's servers. If you tap an app, the App Store opens. As with any internet request, Apple sees the technical details of the connection (such as the IP address) under Apple's own privacy policy; this information does not reach us. In the other 16 apps this list is not shown and this request is not made.

The apps do not connect to ad networks, analytics services, AI services or social networks.

Permissions

The apps do not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or health data permissions; none of them is configured to request these permissions. The one exception is Bayraklar (Flags): if you turn on "Daily reminder" in Settings, iOS asks you for notification permission. The reminder is only a local notification shown inside the device at the time you choose; it is not sent from a server and sends no data to us. The other apps do not ask for notification permission.

3. Differences between the apps

Vücudumuz: Bedenimi Tanıyorum (My Body)

  • Spoken words: Body part names and short phrases are read aloud on the device by iOS's built-in speech synthesizer. The microphone is not used, no audio is recorded, and no text is sent to us or to a third party.
  • Parent gate: The purchase screen and external links (including this page and Apple's terms) are behind an addition question whose answers are shown in words, so that young children cannot pass it easily.
  • There is no "reset progress" button in the app. To delete data, see section 6.

Matematik Oyunları: 1. Sınıf (Kids Math Games)

  • Scratch area: Children can scribble next to a question with a finger or Apple Pencil. The drawing is not saved, stored or sent; it disappears when cleared or when the next question appears.
  • Home screen widget: The optional widget shares the total star count with the app inside the device. It does not use the internet.
  • Sound and vibration: The device's system sound and haptics are used for right and wrong answers. They can be turned off in Settings.
  • No notifications: We do not send reminder notifications to children. The daily streak is shown on screen only while the app is open.

Yazma Pratiği: Harf Rakam (Writing Practice)

  • Drawings stay on the device: A drawing made with a finger or Apple Pencil is checked in the device's memory to see whether it matches the character. The drawing itself is not saved, stored, shared or sent off the device. Only the result (the star count) is stored.
  • The check runs on the device's own processor. It does not use the internet or any AI service.

The other 18 apps

  • Spoken words (on the device): Hayvanları Tanı, Meyveler & Sebzeler, Dinozorlar, Taşıtları Tanı, Renkler & Şekiller, Sayı Sayma, Nokta Birleştir, Bebek Telefonu, İngilizce Alfabe, İlk Okuma and Saat Öğren read names, letters, syllables or short phrases aloud with iOS's built-in speech synthesizer. The microphone is not used, no audio is recorded, and no text is sent to us or to a third party. Karşıtlar, Çocuk Labirenti, Mini Piyano, Boyama Oyunu, Genel Kültür Kids, Çarpım Tablosu and Bayraklar have no spoken words.
  • Mini Piyano and Bebek Telefonu: The piano notes are single tones generated inside the device (there are no downloaded sound files). In Bebek Telefonu the key tones are iOS system sounds.
  • Drawing and touch with a finger: The digit-writing practice in Sayı Sayma is checked in the device's memory; the drawing is not saved and only the star count earned is stored. Following the path with a finger in Çocuk Labirenti, tapping dots in order in Nokta Birleştir and tap-to-color in Boyama Oyunu stay only on the screen and in memory; none of it is saved, exported to photos, shared or sent.
  • Haptic feedback: The apps may use the device's own vibration motor on right and wrong answers. This information does not leave the device.
  • Parent gate: In the 17 apps other than Bayraklar, the purchase screen and external links (this page, Apple's terms of use, the open-source licenses page) are behind a parent gate. In thirteen apps the gate asks an addition question whose answers are shown in words; in four apps (İngilizce Alfabe, İlk Okuma, Genel Kültür Kids, Çarpım Tablosu) it asks a multiplication question, and in these four the Settings screen is behind the gate as well. Bayraklar has no parent gate; a purchase is completed with Apple's system confirmation.
  • Bayraklar: The optional daily reminder is a local notification (section 2, Permissions). The "Delete all data" button in Settings deletes XP, days played, statistics, wrongly answered flags, the quiz count and achievements. The "Our other apps" list appears in this app without a parent gate.
  • Apps with a reset button: In Settings of İngilizce Alfabe ("the learned letter marks will be deleted"), İlk Okuma ("all read cards will be deleted"), Genel Kültür Kids ("scores and statistics will be deleted") and Çarpım Tablosu ("scores, memorised marks and statistics will be deleted") there is a "Reset progress" button. The other 13 apps have no in-app reset button; to delete data, see section 6.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Part of each of the 21 apps is free. Optional auto-renewing subscriptions (weekly, monthly, yearly) that unlock all content are sold through Apple's App Store system (StoreKit).

  • Payment, billing and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple. Card, Apple ID or payment details never reach us.
  • Whether a subscription is active is checked on the device from verified information provided by Apple. We have no subscription server and no third-party subscription service (such as RevenueCat).
  • To cancel or manage a subscription, open Settings > your Apple ID > Subscriptions on the iPhone or iPad.
  • Apple may give developers aggregate sales reports and, if you agreed to share them on your device, usage and crash reports. We cannot identify an individual child from these.
  • In Vücudumuz and in the 17 apps with a parent gate listed above, the purchase screen is behind the parent gate. In Matematik Oyunları, Yazma Pratiği and Bayraklar a purchase is completed with Apple's own confirmation (Face ID, Touch ID or Apple ID password). You can turn off in-app purchases on a child's device under Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > iTunes & App Store Purchases.

5. Children's privacy

The apps are designed for children and are rated 4+ on the App Store. They are built in the spirit of rules that protect children's personal data (such as COPPA in the United States and KVKK in Türkiye), on these principles:

  • A child is never asked for a name, age, photo, voice, location or contact details.
  • There are no ads, behavioural tracking, profiling, social features or chat for children.
  • Because data is not sent to us, there is no knowing collection of personal data from children under 13.
  • For teachers and schools: a student's progress is not sent to the school, the teacher or us; it stays on that iPad. There is no teacher dashboard or class account.

6. Retention, deletion and your rights

  • Retention: Data stays on the device until you delete it. We hold no record of it.
  • Deletion: Removing the app from the device deletes all of its data on that device. In Matematik Oyunları, Yazma Pratiği, İngilizce Alfabe, İlk Okuma, Genel Kültür Kids and Çarpım Tablosu the "Reset progress" button in Settings also deletes progress; the confirmation dialog of that button says which information is deleted. In Bayraklar the "Delete all data" button does the same. You can turn off the daily reminder in Bayraklar in Settings and withdraw the notification permission in iOS Settings > Notifications. Removing the app is the most complete way to delete everything.
  • Backups: If your iPhone or iPad backup (iCloud or computer) is turned on, the app's data on the device may be part of Apple's system backup. We cannot access that backup; it is managed through your Apple account.
  • Your rights: For rights such as asking whether we hold data about you, correction and deletion (in Türkiye, KVKK Article 11), you can write to info@dijitalpilot.com. Because we do not have access to personal data in these apps, we will usually explain that the data is on your device and that you can delete it yourself as described above.

7. Changes

If an update adds a feature that collects real data (for example an account, cloud sync or analytics), we will first update this page and the privacy declaration on the App Store. The "Last updated" date at the top will change. This page is for information and is not legal advice.
