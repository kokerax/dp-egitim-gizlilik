Educational Apps Privacy Policy

Vücudumuz (My Body), Matematik Oyunları (Kids Math Games) and Yazma Pratiği (Writing Practice) · Last updated: October 2, 2026

In short

  • You do not need to create an account or give a name, email address, age or phone number. We do not ask for them.
  • A child's progress (stars, levels, settings) is kept only on that device. It is not sent to us or to the school.
  • No ads. No tracking. No analytics and no third-party software development kits (SDKs).
  • We do not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notification permissions.
  • Subscription payment and management are handled entirely by Apple. Card details never reach us.

1. Which apps does this policy cover?

This page applies to the three educational apps below, published by Dijital Pilot Reklam Sanayi Ticaret Limited Şirketi ("we", "us"). Our other apps have their own privacy policies.

2. What is the same in all three apps

Data we collect

None of the three apps connects to a server, account system or database that we operate, and none sends us personal data. Our App Store "App Privacy" declaration for all three apps is Data Not Collected.

Data that stays on the device

To work, the apps keep the information below only inside the device, in the app's own storage. It does not reach us.

AppInformation stored on the device
VücudumuzNames of the body parts learned, number of stars earned, highest game score, whether the welcome screens were completed, sound on/off setting
Matematik OyunlarıBest star count per level, daily practice streak (last day played, longest streak, total days), a list of questions answered wrongly (a "mistake notebook": operation type, numbers, number of misses, last date), difficulty mode, sound and vibration setting, whether the welcome screens were completed
Yazma PratiğiBest star count per letter, number and shape, whether the welcome screens were completed, whether the first drawing screen was shown, and a history of the last 500 practice attempts (which character, how many stars, which date; this feature is in newer versions)

None of this contains the child's name, age, face, voice, location or a device identifier. If several children use the same app on one device (for example a shared school iPad), their progress is merged into one record. The apps have no separate child profiles.

Internet connection

The apps can be played without internet. The internet is used only to show subscription options, complete a purchase and restore purchases through Apple's App Store service, and for links you tap yourself (this privacy page, Apple's terms of use). The apps do not connect to ad networks, analytics services, AI services or social networks.

Permissions

The apps do not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, health data or notification permissions. None of them is configured to request these permissions.

3. Differences between the apps

Vücudumuz: Bedenimi Tanıyorum (My Body)

  • Spoken words: Body part names and short phrases are read aloud on the device by iOS's built-in speech synthesizer. The microphone is not used, no audio is recorded, and no text is sent to us or to a third party.
  • Parent gate: The purchase screen and external links (including this page and Apple's terms) are behind an addition question whose answers are shown in words, so that young children cannot pass it easily.
  • There is no "reset progress" button in the app. To delete data, see section 6.

Matematik Oyunları: 1. Sınıf (Kids Math Games)

  • Scratch area: Children can scribble next to a question with a finger or Apple Pencil. The drawing is not saved, stored or sent; it disappears when cleared or when the next question appears.
  • Home screen widget: The optional widget shares the total star count with the app inside the device. It does not use the internet.
  • Sound and vibration: The device's system sound and haptics are used for right and wrong answers. They can be turned off in Settings.
  • No notifications: We do not send reminder notifications to children. The daily streak is shown on screen only while the app is open.

Yazma Pratiği: Harf Rakam (Writing Practice)

  • Drawings stay on the device: A drawing made with a finger or Apple Pencil is checked in the device's memory to see whether it matches the character. The drawing itself is not saved, stored, shared or sent off the device. Only the result (the star count) is stored.
  • The check runs on the device's own processor. It does not use the internet or any AI service.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Part of each app is free. Optional auto-renewing subscriptions (weekly, monthly, yearly) that unlock all content are sold through Apple's App Store system (StoreKit).

  • Payment, billing and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple. Card, Apple ID or payment details never reach us.
  • Whether a subscription is active is checked on the device from verified information provided by Apple. We have no subscription server and no third-party subscription service (such as RevenueCat).
  • To cancel or manage a subscription, open Settings > your Apple ID > Subscriptions on the iPhone or iPad.
  • Apple may give developers aggregate sales reports and, if you agreed to share them on your device, usage and crash reports. We cannot identify an individual child from these.
  • In Vücudumuz the purchase screen is behind the parent gate. In Matematik Oyunları and Yazma Pratiği a purchase is completed with Apple's own confirmation (Face ID, Touch ID or Apple ID password). You can turn off in-app purchases on a child's device under Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > iTunes & App Store Purchases.

5. Children's privacy

The apps are designed for children and are rated 4+ on the App Store. They are built in the spirit of rules that protect children's personal data (such as COPPA in the United States and KVKK in Türkiye), on these principles:

  • A child is never asked for a name, age, photo, voice, location or contact details.
  • There are no ads, behavioural tracking, profiling, social features or chat for children.
  • Because data is not sent to us, there is no knowing collection of personal data from children under 13.
  • For teachers and schools: a student's progress is not sent to the school, the teacher or us; it stays on that iPad. There is no teacher dashboard or class account.

6. Retention, deletion and your rights

  • Retention: Data stays on the device until you delete it. We hold no record of it.
  • Deletion: Removing the app from the device deletes all of its data on that device. In Matematik Oyunları and Yazma Pratiği the "Reset progress" button in Settings also deletes progress; the confirmation dialog of that button says which information is deleted. Removing the app is the most complete way to delete everything.
  • Backups: If your iPhone or iPad backup (iCloud or computer) is turned on, the app's data on the device may be part of Apple's system backup. We cannot access that backup; it is managed through your Apple account.
  • Your rights: For rights such as asking whether we hold data about you, correction and deletion (in Türkiye, KVKK Article 11), you can write to info@dijitalpilot.com. Because we do not have access to personal data in these apps, we will usually explain that the data is on your device and that you can delete it yourself as described above.

7. Changes

If an update adds a feature that collects real data (for example an account, cloud sync or analytics), we will first update this page and the privacy declaration on the App Store. The "Last updated" date at the top will change. This page is for information and is not legal advice.
